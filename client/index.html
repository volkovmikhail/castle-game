    Settings

    Scroll speed

    How fast the map moves when you pan with the mouse wheel or trackpad. Default: 0.4.

    How to play

    Objective

    Online battle for 2–4 players — last castle standing wins. Send your knights to destroy enemy castles. If your own castle (600 HP) falls, you are eliminated. Leaving a match in progress counts as a defeat.

    Resources

    You start with 500 wheat · 500 wood · 1000 gold. Wheat and wood are capped at 500 (+250 per Barn); gold has no limit.

    • Wood — send a knight onto forest to chop it: bushes/saplings 5, trees/spruce 10, big trees 15, logs 50.
    • Gold — send a knight onto rocks to mine 1–2 gold.
    • Wheat — grow farms and harvest ripe fields (+15 wheat each).

    Buildings

    Cost order: wheat · wood · gold. Each building has its own key mechanic.

    • Farm 50 wood · 25 gold

      MechanicGrows through 4 stages, then can be harvested for 15 wheat.

    • Farmhouse 20 wheat · 70 wood · 40 gold

      MechanicAutomatically harvests ripe farms around it — passive wheat.

    • Barn 25 wheat · 80 wood · 45 gold

      MechanicRaises the wheat & wood storage cap by 250 each.

    • House 15 wheat · 60 wood · 35 gold

      MechanicAdds 5 knight slots (base cap is 5 knights).

    • Blacksmith 30 wheat · 90 wood · 55 gold

      MechanicUnlocks army upgrades — each one adds 5 upgrade levels.

    • Market 50 wheat · 50 wood · 50 gold

      MechanicTrade resources for gold and back. Only one per player.

    • Knight (train) 25 wheat · 25 gold

      MechanicYour fighting unit — gathers resources and attacks enemies.

    Knights & combat

    • Base knight: 40 HP · 15 damage. Cap is 5 knights + 5 per House.
    • Knights auto-attack enemy knights within 2 tiles. A move-only order (hold E) walks them there without attacking.
    • Move knights onto trees, rocks or ripe farms to gather; onto enemy buildings to attack them (15 damage per hit; buildings regenerate 10 HP/s).

    Blacksmith upgrades

    Each Blacksmith grants +5 upgrade levels for your whole army. Health: +10 HP per level. Attack: +5 damage per level.

    Castle cannon

    Your castle auto-fires at enemy knights within 3 tiles — 50 damage, 1 shot per second. Upgrade it: Range +1 tile (max 5), Damage +15 (no limit), Fire rate −0.1 s per level (max 5).

    Market rates

    10 wheat or 10 wood → 1 gold. 1 gold → 8 wheat or 8 wood.

    Mouse

    • Left click — select a knight, place a building/knight, or open your building's panel.
    • Left drag on empty ground — pan the camera.
    • Shift + drag or right drag — box-select troops.
    • Right click — order selected troops to move / attack there.
    • Mouse wheel / trackpad — pan the map. Hold Space — show who owns what.
